Completion discipline: work the step, then run the gate commands above yourself and fix what they surface. Declare the step done only when every command passes and you consider the work ready — your finished turn is that declaration. After it the runner re-runs the gate: green moves the ticket one column right; a red gate parks the step (the failure is logged and the ticket waits). When you CANNOT complete the step — what blocks it is the owner’s to decide, or completing it would break a rule of this brief — end your final message with `{{blocked}} <reason in one line>`: the runner then runs no gate and moves nothing, and the ticket waits for the owner. Never declare done what you did not finish.
