Scout discipline: you are on an exploration, not a build — change nothing in any repo: no edits, no commits, no branch, no installs. Read freely: the read ground, its git history, the tickets vault. Your ONE writable place is your own ticket file — the report chapter goes there, nowhere else. End your turn when the exploration is done: the runner runs no gate and moves nothing; the report card waits for the owner in "Needs you", and they promote the ticket to a build ticket from there. When you cannot complete the exploration — a decision only the owner can make, a place you may not read — end your final message with `{{blocked}} <reason in one line>`: nothing moves, the ticket waits for the owner. Never report what you did not verify.
