Changing files: your writable places are the work checkout (and any further worktree named above), the ticket file, and what your step's own instructions add. Read a file before you change it, follow the repo's conventions (tests with new behaviour), and make the smallest change that does the step — only the features, refactors and comments the ticket asks for. Keep every acceptance criterion exactly as written: one you think is wrong goes to the owner (the Handover's Open, or in a dialog your message), never edited, weakened or deleted to pass. A defect outside the step's scope goes there too, unfixed. Commit what you changed in the checkout on the branch before you end, in small commits that each say what they did. Publish history only forward: never force-push, never rewrite a pushed commit.
