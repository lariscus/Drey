- **Tranches and checkpoint reports dropped** (owner, 2026-11-15; req-309, which replaces the discarded tranche-fix decision - the id req-308 now belongs to milestone 281's project filter). The Autopilot no longer works in batches (req-179, milestone 221: runs / budget share / minutes, a checkpoint pause with a report, early-stop rules). - **Shape as a held harness session** (owner, 2026-11-14: "Brücke behalten"; req-229, measured in the E10-5b session, milestone 275). Milestone 255's persona voice for Shape stays. If the cost overview matters later, counting Shape turns on the persona's ledger is the smaller item.
