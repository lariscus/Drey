Runner event stream

Goal

The runner reports what happens in a run as it happens, so no client polls for status.

Architecture

Event kinds

started, tool call, silent for N minutes, gate red, done, and quota: a slot's remaining share and reset time after every turn. A quota event is sent at most once a minute per slot.

Clients subscribe through the engine

Drey, Mission Control and the phone client never open the runner's stream. The engine subscribes once and relays the events on its own /api/events.

Assistant
Clients should not talk to the runner directly. Route them through the engine and update the diagram.
